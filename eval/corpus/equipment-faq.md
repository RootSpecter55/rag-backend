# Equipment FAQ (fictional company "Northwind Labs")

New employees receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset on their first day.

Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if a repair would cost more than half the price of a new one.

Employees working from home can claim a one-off home office budget for a desk and chair, ordered through the equipment portal.

Returned equipment must be shipped back within ten working days of leaving the company, using the prepaid label from IT.
