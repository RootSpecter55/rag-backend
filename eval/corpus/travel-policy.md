# Travel policy (fictional company "Northwind Labs")

Flights must be booked through the travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made later need written approval from a department head.

Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the nightly city rate shown in the travel portal. Anything above that rate is paid by the employee.

Meal expenses are covered up to a daily allowance; receipts must be uploaded within 30 days of returning.
