# Leave policy (fictional company "Northwind Labs")

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.

Up to five unused leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for any parent, and can be taken within the first year after a birth or adoption.

Sick leave does not reduce annual leave. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than three consecutive working days.
